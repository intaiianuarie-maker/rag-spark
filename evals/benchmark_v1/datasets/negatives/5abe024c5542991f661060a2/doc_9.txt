Steve Potter

Steve Potter is a former footballer.  Born Stephen Derek Potter in 1955 in Belper, Derbyshire, Steve was apprenticed at age 15 with Manchester City Football Club.  He transferred to Swansea City and made 118 appearances as a goalkeeper for Swansea City between 1974 and 1978 before moving to Bridgend Town in July 1978.  After a short stint with Bridgend to maintain fitness, Steve moved to Vancouver, Canada, to play for the Vancourver White Caps Football Club in the then bourgening North American League.  He received an offer to play for a Melbourne club and subsequently emigrated to Australia.  Steve retired in the early 1980s and now lives in Noosa Heads, Queensland.  Proud father to mega fox, Shae Potter.
