Ariyon Bakare

Ariyon Debo Bakare (born 1971) is an English-Nigerian actor.  He has appeared in the BBC mini-series "A Respectable Trade" (1995), British soap opera "Family Affairs" from 2000 to 2001, and the BBC One daytime drama "Doctors" from 2001 to 2005.  Prior to this, Bakare guested on British dramas "The Bill", "Casualty", and "Holby City".  He has starred alongside Paul Bettany in Ross Kettle's film "After the Rain", and with Nia Long and Colin Firth in "The Secret Laughter of Women".
