Total South Africa

Total South Africa, headquartered in Rosebank, South Africa, is an energy company that manufactures and sells a full range of petroleum products to the retail, commercial and industrial markets in South Africa.  It is part of the French-based multinational corporation, Total S.A. (Euronext: FP, NYSE: TOT), one of the six "Supermajor" oil companies in the world.  Total South Africa has a network of 530 service stations throughout South Africa, and is a key player in the petrochemicals market with products ranging from jet fuel and liquefied petroleum gas to lubricants, greases and kerosene.
