Ahmed Şerafettin

Ahmed Şeraffettin Bey or Şeref Bey (1894–13 June 1933) was a Turkish football manager.  He was also the first manager of the Turkish club Beşiktaş J.K. (1911–1925), as well as one of the team's icons.  He played a very important role for the club, bringing them to three league championships (1920, 1921, 1924).  Şeref Bey started the football division of Beşiktaş.
