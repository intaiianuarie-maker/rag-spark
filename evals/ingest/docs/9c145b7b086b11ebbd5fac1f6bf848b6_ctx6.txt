Terry Bishop

Terry Bishop( 21 October 1912- 30 October 1981) was a British screenwriter, and television and film director. During the 1950s and 60s he worked extensively in British TV, directing episodes of series such as" The Adventures of William Tell The Adventures of Robin HoodSword of Freedom Danger Man", and" Sir Francis Drake". He also made several low budget British films during this time, perhaps the best known of which is" Cover Girl Killer", starring" Steptoe and Son ’s" Harry H. Corbett as a serial murderer.
