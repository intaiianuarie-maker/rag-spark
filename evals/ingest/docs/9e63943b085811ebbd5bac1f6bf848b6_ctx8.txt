Aasha (1983 film)

Aasha( Kannada: ಆಶಾ) is a 1983 Indian Kannada film, directed by A. T. Raghu and produced by R. F. Manik Chand. The film stars Ambarish, Arjun Sarja, Kumari Indira and Kalaranjini in the lead roles. The film has musical score by G. K. Venkatesh. The film became a superhit. Ambareesh went on to star in a 1991 movie with similar storyline titled" Garuda Dhwaja". The movie was remade in Hindi in 1984 as" Meri Adalat" starring Rajinikanth and in Malayalam in 1984 as" Ivide Thudangunnu" starring Mohanlal.
