Ingegerd Birgersdotter of Bjelbo

Ingegerd( or" Ingegärd") Birgersdotter of Bjelbo( or" Bjälbo"; ca. 1180 – 7 April after 1210, possibly 1230) was a Swedish Queen Consort, second spouse of King Sverker II of Sweden. Ingegerd was born into one of Sweden's most powerful noble families, the Bjälbo family, as the child of the powerful Jarl Birger Brosa and the Queen Dowager Brigida Haraldsdotter; her mother was the child of King Harald Gille of Norway and had, in her first marriage, herself been the Queen of Sweden in 1160 – 1161.
