Cailee Spaeny

Cailee Spaeny( born July 24, 1997) is an American actress and singer. Her debut film role was as Erica in the 2016 short film" Counting to 1000". In 2018, she played several major film roles, starring in Steven S. DeKnight's science fiction monster adventure, alongside John Boyega and Scott Eastwood; Drew Goddard's thriller" Bad Times at the El Royale"; and the biographical dramas" On the Basis of Sex" and" Vice". She will next appear in" The Shoes" and the upcoming remake of" The Craft".
