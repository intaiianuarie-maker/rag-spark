Princess Isabelle of Isenburg

Princess Isabelle of Wied( born 1973) previously Princess of Isenburg is third child and second daughter of Franz Alexander, Prince of Isenburg, head of the mediatized House of Isenburg and his wife, Countess Christine von Saurma- Jelltsch. By marriage Princess Isabelle was the wife of the Head of the House of Wied- Neuwied. Once widowed that position went to her eldest son Maximilian, 9th Prince of Wied.
