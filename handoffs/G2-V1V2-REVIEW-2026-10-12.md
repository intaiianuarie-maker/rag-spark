## 6. Remaining non-blocking observations (do not block merge)

- **N1 — duplicate entities from the rule extractor.** A single chunk mentioning
  `Acme Corp` twice yields two `Acme Corp` entities (`['Acme Corp','Acme Corp','Initech']`)
  with different ids. The existing `SimpleEntityResolver` exists to merge these;
  `RuleBasedGraphExtractor` appears not to run it by default in this path. Worth a
  follow-up card on entity dedup quality — not a correctness/security defect, and
  downstream retrieval is unaffected.
- **N2 — silent construction fallback.** `factory.py` wraps `PostgresChunkLookup()`
  in `except Exception: pass`. Fail-closed for citations (correct), but a Postgres
  outage is indistinguishable in telemetry from "no graph results". A warning log
  would aid diagnosis.
- **N3 — broad exception in the lookup adapter.** `PostgresChunkLookup.get()`
  catches `Exception` → `None` (logged at warning). Fail-closed and acceptable.
- **N4 — test-naming accuracy.** `test_graph_indexing_hook_executed_when_enabled`
  now passes, but it exercises `RuleBasedGraphExtractor` directly rather than
  through `process_job`. The C1 defect was still invisible to it. A
  `process_job`-level test would give real regression coverage of the hook itself.
  (My C1 runtime probe covers this gap; a permanent test would be better.)
